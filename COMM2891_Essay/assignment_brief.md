# COMM2891 Asian Media and Communication — Assignment 1: Major Research Essay (brief as supplied by the student)

## Task
Write a research essay on one of three topics. Explain your answer with reference to the various media models and country-specific analyses of the media environment discussed in the course.

Chosen topic — Japan's Treated Fukushima Nuclear Wastewater Discharge:
Investigate Japan's controversial plan to discharge treated Fukushima nuclear wastewater into the Pacific and the reaction within Japan and internationally.

QUESTION: How does the media coverage (including traditional and emerging media) of this issue in Japan compare with some of its Asian neighbours? What are the reasons for the similarities and disparities you have identified?

Word limit: 2000 words (student-specified; the brief itself states none). References excluded.

## Key requirements
- Clearly state research focus and scope (case study countries).
- Contextualise the topic using concepts/theoretical approaches from the course (media models, e.g. Hallin & Mancini; Four Theories of the Press; country-specific media-environment analyses).
- Investigate thoroughly, then analyse with sufficient evidence from own research and other researchers; well-supported key points and conclusions based on logical reasoning.
- At least FIVE relevant academic references, read, discussed and cited. Non-academic sources (reports, news, government, NGOs, think tanks) may also be used.
- Suggested structure: Introduction (introduce topic; explain how it will be addressed; clarify key concepts with operational definitions; preview findings) → Body (answer the question in a logical sequence; evidence; place topic in course context; critical academic reasoning) → Conclusion (clear summary; answer the research question).
- Explain key concepts with an operational (working) definition.
- APA 7 referencing, in text and in a reference list.
- Format: title page with course code and title (COMM2891 Asian Media and Communication), student name, RMIT student number, contact details, tutorial class and tutor's name; Arial or Times New Roman 12; double-spaced throughout; first line of every paragraph indented; page numbers; default margins; word count listed at the end of the essay, before the reference list.

## Rubric (100 points)
- Criterion 1 — Research (30%). HD (24–30): accurate and thorough understanding of topic and requirements; demonstrated solid research; clarity of research scope and concepts used; demonstrated originality of thought.
- Criterion 2 — Analysis (30%). HD (24–30): familiarity with the research literature based on at least 5 highly relevant academic sources; application of relevant theoretical approaches; sophisticated analysis based on logical reasoning; ability to engage in debate; demonstrated knowledge of academic integrity.
- Criterion 3 — Structure and Expression (20%). HD (16–20): clear and coherent logical structure; expresses and develops ideas; discussion and conclusion with solid evidence; excellently meets all technical aspects of essay.
- Criterion 4 — Communication and Referencing (20%). HD (16–20): clear, unambiguous language; well written, consistent correct grammar, well edited; references consistently conform to APA 7.
Lower bands: D 70–79%, C 60–69%, P 50–59%, NS <50%.
